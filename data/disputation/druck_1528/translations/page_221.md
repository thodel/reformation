**Schlußred.**  

The flesh and blood, truly partaking.  Further in the afore‑cited chapter of John, chapter 5, it says that he who eats my flesh and drinks my blood cannot enter the kingdom of heaven.  Yet where my flesh is and my blood is poured in me, and I am in Him, yet where I am, He also loves because of my (self).  Here is shown a necessary food which is not harmful to anyone, but in every way useful and good.  But the food in the Lord’s night‑meal is not forced, not in every way useful, but rather somewhat harmful.  It is also contrary to our old learned belief, which says: He is ascended to the heavens; He sits at the right hand of the Father; He will come to judge, living and dead.  

As we clearly see in the histories of the Apostles, in the first chapter: the men of Galilee … and Matthew in chapters 24 and 25, from all this follows this word: Taking and eating, that is my life.  Likewise, from the drink another understanding must be had; then we give a hand that has been given before, except that such is nothing else than a praise and thanksgiving of the death and redemption of Christ, as later will be obtained through Paul in the first Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter 10 and 12.  

**Benedictus Burgouwer, pfarrer**
