**Schlußred. CLXXIII**

**Johannes Buͦchstab.**

In the name of the holy Dreifaltigkeit and all the heavenly hosts. Amen. We find by the prophet David, Psalm 109, as it is said to the Hebrews in chapter VII, that Christ is forever a priest after the order of Melchizedek. The same Melchizedek, a king of Salem, has Abraham brought bread and wine: in his giving and speaking: “May Abraham be blessed by the most high God who created heaven and earth.” Genesis xiiij. Thus now the bread and wine that were brought are a figure of Christ’s body. As shown above, so must also follow that Christ also needed to have made himself like Melchizedek. And as Melchizedek had Abraham bring bread and wine as an offering, so far the body of Christ in the form of the bread has been offered.

**Berchtoldus Haller.**

While Melchizedek may be a figure of Christ and Christ a priest after the order of Melchizedek, as Paul writes to the Hebrews in chapter VII, nevertheless Melchizedek had Abraham bring bread and wine as an offering; the text does not allow this (Genesis xiiij). It also has not permitted Abraham that the rest of the priest of God, as a creature, should offer. Therefore the Scripture is to be understood that Melchizedek’s bread and wine were brought as an offering and thus serve such an argument, not at all contrary to the Schlussrede.
