Closing Speech CXCIII  

Maccabeus in the fourth chapter, with the words: “As Judas Maccabeus spoke to the people: ‘Leaving us to call upon heaven, so God will have mercy on us; first give … as Judas has not spoken. Leaving us to call upon God, rather than to heaven, so God will have mercy on us; He has indeed left it, that it is not unjust to call upon God also; He has, however, the people commanded, to call upon heaven; but what the heavens are, He guards according to Psalm XVIII. The heavens proclaim Your praise, because He thinks that they are the saints or holy ones in heaven; therefore I am uncertain whether He still holds that opinion.’”

Herr Johan Lottstetter Kilcher to Brugk.  

[D] Jewyl and I am **erworben** (appointed) by the Biedermann, answer to give, my preaching half, so on All Saints’ Day to Brugk, to the Christian people to be announced, meeting the highly praised young women Marie and all God’s holy prayers. Whether I forgive them again or not, I answer as I have learned before; thus I forgive them again. I have therefore, on the holy common Christian church, also before my gracious lord (mandate left), and I think I have acted Christianly and obediently. Herewith I bring it to dispute, the more I have learned, that I am … to those with whom I have corresponded.  

Hanns Wåchter
