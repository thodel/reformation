**Schlußred.**  
Scripture, gentlemen/ and this certain saying/ so it was indeed  
greatly enough to preserve the distribution of the love and  
the blood of Christ in the Eucharist: I am also certain that all  
will not wish the saying to become: it stands for us/ a people also:  
The cup or the tract of the blessing that we bless is not the  
communion of the blood of Christ: and the bread that we break is not the  
communion of the love of Christ: All know that a small understanding of the Greek word κοινωνία, that is, a communion, a distribution, exists. As Romans xv, Paul in the same form of the word uses, so he says: It has been regarded from Macedonia and Achaia as good, a communion together in the poor holy ones who are in Jerusalem. What is communion there else than a distribution that they wanted to be helpful to the poor. In the first Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter viii, again κοινωνία is taken for a distribution. Thus also here the cup, that saying or designation, is a communion; that is a distribution of the blood; and the bread that we break is a distribution of the life of Christ. Can my Lord Socrates compel me with writings to a different understanding, so we will hear him.
