**Berchtoldus.**  

There is a single head, which is a holy land of the community of God, as Paul says in the Epistles, chapter five. There are many offices or appeals; they are members of the love of Christ, but they are not the head about which Scripture speaks, the one that makes the members alive, and a holy land is of life. Therefore the pastor may not argue against our conclusion, that although the apostles are lighter than the world, they are not essentially so, but those who have fled from the head, Christ, as do all believers, all Christians. From this, however, it does not follow that all Christians are chiefly the church. Bringing clear Scripture, that another head of the church is, which makes the members alive, and its holy land is.  

**Meister Vlrich Zwingli/**  

I desire a little explanation, how Christ bears the light, and how the apostles speak of bearing the light: Christ is the true essential light, John in the first chapter, and all the light that the apostles have, he gives to one. Taking, dear Lord Pastor, an example: The sun gives the day through the window, and it is the day or light useful for itself.
