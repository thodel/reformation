Schlußred.  

We ask that it be noted how the pious people, who act in a well‑doing manner, praise God and honor Him, and keep in mind the holy goodwill.  

We also have II Reg. viii cap.  

God has performed a great miracle in the Old Testament through the dead Helios, who was not yet blessed, as our holy ones are blessed in the New Testament; for a dead body was cast into the grave of Helios and, by the prayers of the believers, has become alive again: thereby one may perceive that God works through the signs of the dead.  

With this, I cast myself, the universal Christian Church, to show where I would not be uniform, being inclined toward the divine word.  

Zwingli.  

Since the worldly blessed obedience and a holy will of God, we recognize that we ask that God’s will be fulfilled, as in heaven, where no one sets themselves against God’s will.  

But this is being introduced, so also pray for us; it is not right, however, that we have already been permitted to speak of Christ’s forgiveness; nevertheless we ask only for the sake of good understanding.  

Not that it be otherwise, nor do we yet hear in the words of John, chapter ii, that thus stands.  

My child, I also write these things, that you do not sin; but if anyone sins, we have an intercessor or trustee from the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous, who is gracious toward our sin.  

Here we note that Christ does not plead for us with fearful battles or downfall, as we might ask to understand, but that his prayer was once heard (as the Hebrews …). He is, at the time of his flesh, dead.
