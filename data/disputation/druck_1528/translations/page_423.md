Schlußred.  

We did not know which Gospel we ought to keep, yet it is claimed that the well‑known Zechar has written the Gospel. And those who say that the Apocalypse is not of John the Evangelist, also that the Epistle to the Hebrews is not of Paul, that which Epistle our party, according to its understanding, has on every ground against the Mass, that it is not a sacrifice.  

Meyster Vlrich Zwingli  

We would gladly use the Christian church, but each in his own way. There are books in the Old Testament which the children of Israel in the sum and the number of the holy Scripture have never taken up, preserved, as has been spoken through the Ecumenical Council. Likewise there are also in the New Testament. The preservation of the church is not the preservation of the coming bishops, but the preservation of all truly faithful. The books, the knowledge of which we do not take in the preservation of the hard dealings of the faith, we do not reject, just as the women in the church are not to be rejected. So, if one is to treat matters seriously, one must not take the knowledge away.  

Thus this Disputation, which has been held in such great esteem, after the breaking of all learned and God‑fearing things, let only the knowledge remain that no one may reject. Not that in the Scriptures they complain, that it is useful, that the preacher’s closing remarks should be weakened, but that a certain order be kept, as previously stated. As for the Epistle to the Hebrews, we say that we have left it to every one here, whoever has written it. I, for my own person, do not recognize otherwise.
