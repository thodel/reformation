The first that I have drawn from Holy Scripture for an equal Christian reader for my person.  

Zwingli.  

[I] I have not taken the parable, that I would base the divine word upon, except the singular, which is well known, as also the holy Paul has said to the Romans in the first chapter, to give illumination, that the divine word of this first [haller] is first. Accordingly the pastor recognises God the Lord Jesus Christ, his keeper, and of all the world, also a chief one. These are all the words of the pastor, as they are here presented. Hereby I praise the God who has promised that, if one asks, he will raise up, that he on the appointed day has heard the devout Christians of Bern, who in those days so often anxiously begged, whose hearts are most eager, that our dear pastor and brothers of Alpentzell recognise that Christ the Lord is their keeper, also the keeper of all the world: then, so far and thus by right, we have spoken in belief.
