Berchtoldus.  
You have, after a long time, preserved that Christ is the one head of his churches, teaches, governs, leads, and brings to a virtuous life, that no creature is able, but nevertheless he uses an appointed steward, by the power of the following, to try it with Scripture; then that is Christ’s judgment.
