Seite XI  
The first thing that comes to hand / besides it is to recognize the Spirit and the Scripture / whether it has been written by the Spirit of God / which the opposition cannot alone refute / nor may the holy Christian church / if it nevertheless has itself been led / and taken up / then there are several books / of the holy Scripture / highly praised / honored and prized / as those that are wholly filled with the Spirit and the Gospel / as the Epistles to the Romans / the Gospel of John / etc. and other writings / whether they have been for a thousand years regarded by the church as holy / diminished and corrupted / as the Epistle of James / Apocalypse / and the like / why then should they have no force and power / the whole common / holy Christian church / the Spirit of the Scripture judges / and declares what has been written / as the high reverend Joannes says. Test the Spirit / whether it is from God / here my lord Doctor cannot deny / that the Scripture may be judged (as is proper) / may become. Moreover / concerning the saying (Matthew that the church does not hear / etc.) my lord Doctor answered / that such a saying should be understood from sins / namely that a brother sins against another / not from belief / then the Scripture should be the judge therein. And how indeed the bare words are / my lord Doctor’s / and with your writing shown / therefore then sufficient / no / moreover I say / otherwise the text is not clear / nor is it understood / therefore it also in your other ... (the passage ends).
