Diligently answered / on the first sermon Lord Benedicts / I say that in all interpretations of Scripture we ought to rightly name what is before and after God / and thus with the interpretation of the ban we should …  
Now it is evident that here in this chapter the discourse is held concerning the eating of the flesh of Christ.  
Therefore this word also bears upon the same kind of explanation, and likewise Christ speaks of his flesh; thus here in these words his flesh is not denied, yet we will examine the text.  
It was a warning among the youths of the Lord; because of the spoken word they nevertheless understood little of it; after that they received the understanding of Christ; thus Christ explains his word further, and says, “in a question, know.”  
Consider this: it means that he has spoken of his flesh, that it should be given; he sets a short broken question; whereby you see the Son of Man firmly … because he was … and fulfills the word not … will … say … so I go to heaven, for you also … as the world says … freely … unless you receive the holy spirit … you will rightly recognize … also other teachings … as all … as all comfort … as satisfaction … that was love standing in the trust in Christ … that was heaven.
