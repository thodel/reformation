Half of the essential … your year may arise from it. That he withdrew, contrary to the saying “the flesh is no benefit,” is explained and accountable. That he withdrew, that the flesh and the blood have not been revealed to you, nor likewise the Scripture or the dispute turn against us. That he means it is to be feared, that we give too much to faith, that Paul denies faith and love: he still does not understand that Paul’s faith is not the same as the holy James in his Epistle, for the sense or opinion of what is believed, which sense of opinion is much perished by the mouth, but they do not have it in the heart with trust. It is certain that such faith is of no use; then it is not the faith of which one speaks that makes sin: then the same faith is an unhesitating work and a burning of the human heart against God. Thus he becomes guilty of the life and blood of Christ and is also accountable.  

We also do not speak thus that there is any use in the Sacrament; rather, we recognize that in the Sacrament the blessing of the bread and wine, the announcement of Christ’s death, is not concerned only with words but also in our heart; that belongs to the Sacrament of the Night‑Meal.
