XIII  

Christ, in the first epistle, in the other chapter.  

The other saying to us from Matthew in chapter xxii: of the didrachma Petzo gives not, that it be a head, but with this that Christ has shown himself and the churches in Petzo in earthly things, so that it meets well, the worldly authority being under wrath.  

Thus he makes Peter a head, that Christ is one and we are in the earthly things, the authority overthrown, that he to Luke in the chapter reproaches: for the ship into the deep, he himself knows whether it is a miracle, and so he already opens the allegory; indeed that alone is a sign, meaning that he is eager in the final office.

Petro, I have prayed for you, that you may not hear your belief, that we confess with you.  

But the brother follows, strengthens your brother.  

For he is so blind, the one who does not see, the service of the servant now is shown sufficiently, that the service is not a head of the churches.  

Also, as he enters in John chapter xxii, Petro, my work is higher and clear, that it is to the service of the word, which is the work’s width: then so wide a head was made, becoming all servants of the word of God, a higher head, that he brings in John chapter yy: they see the foundation confusing.  

And Matthew chapter vvj: what binds them, therefore, the greatest vesture of the bishop is set, not a stronger foundation hard.
