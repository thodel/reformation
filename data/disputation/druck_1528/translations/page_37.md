XI  
In such an external regiment we have been able to prove,  
as one speaks after Paul concerning the Christian community,  
which there find all those now placed in the spirit,  
a divine life for which no one, except the Lord, may forbid,  
and all things are done. Saint Peter and all the Apostles,  
only an external service is taken by Paul; thus it is not yet proved  
that (the Scripture, after the argument) Peter or any other apostle of God  
might be a head of the churches taken, and the bishop should be responsible  
for the saying of Paul in the fourth chapter, 8th verse of the first Epistle to the Corinthians.  
Then Paul writes of him and his like; one should hold them as servants of Christ and preachers,  
that is, preacher of the hiddenness of God. Namely the holy Gospel still stands there; the Scripture brings what he has said, that it may become the head within. Christ is alone the spiritual Lord; he can be other, that they are crooked. Saint Paul, Peter empty, brings nothing; Christ lies then. No one can the heretics thereafter, and giving thus Christ the honor, the land they bind, that is, recognize bound, those who do not believe. Then those are the children of Abraham, those who work Abraham’s thunder; therefore I write so. Thus Peter of his office a head may be taken.
