**Schlußred.**  

Apocalypse is not a Biblical book; we avoid it. Then, wherever the biblical books are printed in the Christian church, it is altogether understood that the book of Apocalypse is a work that the holy Evangelist Saint John himself wrote.  

**Zwingly**  

His syllogism does not follow: Christ is our mediator, intercessor, or prince (Johannes iij). So the saints are also intercessors and mediators; just as only the Son of God suffered death for us, so also He alone may be our sole mediator. The blessed ones before God, by nature and essence not being gods, have not suffered death for us; therefore they are not our mediators either. Hence we speak of mediators. As he says: “Why should they not be alike?” Answer: because He is not God, and they are not; and because God, with His word and spirit, promises Christ as a single mediator, but promises none for the blessed. The book of Apocalypse, being of John the Evangelist, is brought with no other scripture or addition.
