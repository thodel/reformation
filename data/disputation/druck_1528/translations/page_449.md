Schlußred. CCXVII  

Johannes Büchstab.  
The words of Christ are a parable; therefore they are not only for the living who serve, but also for the dead. As he says here concerning punishment, it will also be there. And the punishment of the church is not eternal, as (the) Hell. Thus, having Isaiah xxiii before us, they will be decided in the church, and after many days the afflicted will be found, which literally means that the church would signify an eternal exclusion.  

Ecolampadius.
