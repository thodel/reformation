The first Kirche / the same has also gone higher. So now the figure must be fulfilled / it is necessary / that we also have a superiority / of the righteousness half / in the Christian Kirche. But that alone the Synagoge has a higher priest in all ways / especially two / namely (Moses und Aaron / they must also have something in the new Testament / that the figure will be equally honored: namely Christus und Petrus. I preserve this through the time / Exodus in the fourth chapter / where God speaks to Moses: “Aaron will speak for you to the people / and his mouth will be yours: but you will be his / his mouth in those things that belong to God.” This figure will be fulfilled in Christus und Petrus / that I will preserve according to the holy Evangelist Lukas in the twenty‑second chapter / where God the Lord has spoken: “Simon Simon / [‑] war / Satan has also ordered you / because he also …” but I have prayed for you / that your faith not be … and you are turned back. … Here we have openly / as Moses an upper priest / the Synagoge / against God the Almighty / Also also Christus the highest head is / the whole Christianity / in those things that are being dealt with against God / namely because he has spoken / He would pray for … his faith not … Petrus however will be compared and understood / through the priest Aaron: then as the same recommended what the Israelite people / when Moses went up the mountain Sinai / with God the Almighty to speak / Thus Christus also recommended the holy Saint Petrus / therefore / that he should be a guardian of the people / and spread the office of God the Lord.
