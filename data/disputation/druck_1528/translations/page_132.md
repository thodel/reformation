Seite IIII  
Die erſt Senger.  

When my lord preacher speaks, I have argued that I have called Christ a single head of the church; that Christ was before the church; therefore I say that according to divinity Christ is eternally the head, but according to the flesh he has delivered us with his bitter suffering and death, and has thus made us wretched. In him, if a life is attached to that head, that head is also contrary to it.  

Further, I understand from my lord’s preaching that they have taken a true head in the church, which may make us wretched; but our salvation may be … (the preacher) … not yet a bishop.  

If Lord Berchtold brings this forward with a higher writing, that a head may not be taken in the church, I must expect it. But the holy Paul, in the Epistle to the Corinthians, gives me another understanding of the head; thus he says: “The women should be under their husbands, as a man is the head of a woman, just as Christ is the head of the church; and he is the keeper of his life.”  

Accordingly, if I have fallen in this matter, the Christian church should have a lord‑hand, that it may be defended against oppression, tyranny, and force; I am also of my lord preacher’s opinion.  

Nevertheless, the higher … of the prelates recognized in the church, those who teach, will be … and … I think it is fitting, also for research, that timely necessity creates their works wherever it may be.  

Thus Paul says in the first Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter …: “What do you desire? I will … with the …” (the text breaks off).
