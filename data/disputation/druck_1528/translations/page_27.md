Concluding speech.

This concluding speech understands four articles. The first is: Christ is the head of the holy/common church. When this article is understood by the inner influence of grace and the gracious life, it needs no disputation, because the head is infused in the whole body, the flesh and the movements. Thus the flow to Christ, the head of the churches, in all united members, the grace that is there, the life of the faithful Epistle 1. God has given Christ a head over all churches, the synodists and the perfection of all things, which he has fulfilled in all. Likewise the same place at the fourth chapter we will throw forth the truth and also in its increase through all things the head is, whereby the whole body together is bound and knotted through each one equally the ... (the text is fragmentary) ... thus makes the living body, the building itself in love. Likewise the same place at the fifth chapter: Christ is the head of the churches and he is the healer of the life. The sayings and similar things show that Christ is the source of the churches by the influence of the head in the body and church, says John the Baptist. From his full hand we have all taken grace, when the law is given through Moses, the grace and truth have been accomplished through Jesus Christ and Paul. Epistle III. A certain ... is given grace ... after the measure and gift of Christ ... the sayings and similar things are clear.
