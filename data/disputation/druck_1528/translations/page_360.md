The fifth, concerning marriage / joy / or sorrow / door / tribulation / illness / that they have all with the water‑waves directed rightly.  
Thus, and of the same misuse / what can one not see / that they are terribly dreadful before God: and that the very same is fulfilled / that Christ’s Light. Matthew xviii. says: “How high it is before men / it is a dread before God.”  
Therefore we deem this concluding speech sufficiently justified and clear.  
And with it the truth of this destruction comes to light / and useless words are concealed / so that all those who despise this concluding speech / that we may become a figure: then they with us acknowledge / that it is not justified.  
We will also not accept / that so they would bring a harshness / from the sacrifices of the old Testament / then those already fulfilled in Christ and are fulfilled / as they now have heard / according to the Hebrews in chapter v:  
That we now have a sacrifice / a sacrificer / and an altar / that is Christ / according to Hebrews viii. acknowledging.  
They also will in vain insert / the sayings of the prophets / Isaiah xviii. / Zephaniah ii. / Malachi i. and ii. who then speak of the sacrifices / all Christians in common / then they themselves are our life / according to Romans xii. sacrifice of praise. Psalm i. and service of brotherly love / according to Hebrews xiii.  
We will also no longer hear of the power of the churches.  
Thus by the first and other concluding speeches / enough has been said about it.  
Therefore Paul teaches that the night‑sign should be held for all Christians in common / in the congregation.  
Hence this concluding speech will be refuted / bring clear Scripture / so that the congregation of God with useful words will not be burdened.
