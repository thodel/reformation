Berchtoldus. +  
Stat geschrieben. So should God your lord  
be offered / and him alone serve: because now with  
your scripture against the fourth Schlussrede is brought / that the body and the blood of Christ are truly in the bread of thanksgiving / so it should never be offered: then one would otherwise be offered nothing / then the sacramental bread / would here be not despised / or would corrupt the Sacrament / especially after the blessed Christ / should be held and dealt with.
