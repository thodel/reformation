The first alone, therefore then must follow true love, that all Christians, as one body, be; our opposite party, however, also wishes us against the Pope, against the Councils, which they and they have decided against each other, against the fathers, whose yours is, also not his own doctrine in some places has been altered, we have known; in our doctrine there is neither servant nor lord, neither woman nor man, but all in Christ Jesus; so from our opposite party, spiritual and worldly, or our and priests, the orders also of countless differences, of everything full of sects, of parties that are also deadly to each other, as only between preacher and barefooted of the whole world is known; therefore dear Christians, also leaving with learned words your “Brülle” (brothers) set forth, seeking the saying of Paul to the Romans, xviii chapter, I, however, remind—again to the third, that from anger that has been drawn, I gladly give— that the harsher anger may not be; then the people from the right some belief will be removed; that should never happen by us, but by the opposite party will be invented many times.  

Now to the last, also the whole matter stands there, that through the church they stand, that one according to the word of Christ to the brothers should be shown; it is in the right that it has been justly judged; that such a church is every community and assembly of Christians, which is well‑furnished in flesh but not after the flesh lives; then they have the spirit of Christ; the same now should be shown to the brother who, over the other, has received warning, whose year‑long (i.e., long‑standing) sin has not been removed, lest the community also be chastised and warned where he also despises a holy one, holding him for a heathen, and one who, from the community of God, as the Jews held the taxes of the Romans, the publicans. But my lord doctor has argued against that.
