Closing address.  

Clearly declared / thus he speaks / with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ / therefore all are bound / according to the spirit Christ will be bound / or however it is a force / to devour / the decree of the pastor that a church hearing goes and is entirely godless / and that the Lord Jesus Christ is not recognized / and go to the same church / and say / you having idols and idolatry / you are idolaters / or the same / so the one who has spoken the truth becomes banned / why, therefore, the same church does not have the spirit of Christ / and through it is not governed / thereby it is measured by light / that the power to ban is no human power / except the influence of the one God / where God does not rule the ban with his spirit / thus it is a tyrant and a book / not a betterment or discipline / that he, however, accordingly also desires the saying of Pauli / i. ad Thimoth. i. Cap. which Heaven and Alexander I have given to the Tüffel / so that they may be disciplined, not to be delayed / and think thereby to be preserved. / Paul has alone banned the churches / but that is an error / a misunderstanding of the breaking of Scripture / which in all things needs many synods / that is a kind where one … for the many / or contrary, the many for the … as when one … a … of the assembly of Bern / the … of Bern … contrary … speaks / the … of Bern / or … answer / but not all of Bern / so that the one … also here Paul / I have given them to the Tüffel / not that he has alone banned them / but that he wishes to say / they are … by their fall … from the church / where I have … their fall … I have … / so much I have, dear brothers, from the ban …
