The naked man, without whom it is given. Likewise Paul also speaks in the first Corinthians, chapter 5. Of young women I have no command from the Lord; what will He not permit without the Spirit? Thus also Paul to Timothy, chapter 2. There ought to be a bishop, unapproachable; one woman … who in his own house understands well: the obedient child he has, with all reverence. Likewise also to Titus, chapter 1: Thus the Scripture says: the holy … stands, no one … no one receives, that Christ receives, as reported, and the holy Paul and other attributes of a bishop or pastor, with printed words proclaim the Spirit. So it follows, as Paul himself says, to Timothy, chapter 4. The Spirit offers; it is the devil’s emptiness, of the yearly spirits. From this is judged the terrifying earth of the episcopal regiment. That no one is permitted to the episcopal office; therefore, without the Spirit he is unloved, disregarded, what a life he otherwise would have been. It also says Paul, first Corinthians, chapter 5. That the haste to avoid a certain (nobody receives) his wife: in a certain … man have. And again, those who do not restrain themselves should become filthy; then they are filthy, then they burn loudly. As if he said, the need is driven to the Spirit: if God does not give the gift of purity, without it the body in its first creation may remain; it becomes a certain god‑like … well forced to become filthy; so he aches with good accustomed love will. Thus is the concluding speech, gently illuminated and fortified: also hope that all Christians will be inclined, so that by this God is glorified, a whole Christian community is … and the word of God in many …
