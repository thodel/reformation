**Ecolampadius.**  
He asks Lord Benedict what the Lord has granted to the younger; I answer: the cup or the wine, but not harmful, rather that his suffering with it should be brought to a thanksgiving.  

**Pfarrer von Sant Gallen.**  
As our pledge has been secured on the cross, no one may, according to the words themselves, deny it, just as it is said: “This is my blood, which was shed on the cross,” in the words the Lord spoke: “Drink of it all, this is my blood; I have given it, and let us drink it.” Therefore these words cannot be understood without sense and comprehension; we therefore compose a written instruction, that we may be assured in our conscience of such understanding: then the Lord, with such words, “Drink,” has declared it, as he did on the cross. Concerning the division concerning Peter in the Epistle to the Hebrews, no one may refute it; but do not try to claim that the words “This is my blood” were not the blood of Christ. So anyone who does not believe this sacrament (as it is named) is not planted in Christ through faith.
