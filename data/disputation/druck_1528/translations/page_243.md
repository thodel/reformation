Closing Address CXIII  
Letter.

The word of John in the … chapter indicating the future; the word of Christ, Light on the 22nd, indicating the present event; the word of Paul in the … Epistle to the Corinthians, … chapter, showing clearly the past events. Now the future, the present, and the past, as further shown, are each alike; therefore there is no longer any need for special gloss. But my lord Doctor says that I have brought his doctors forward; I have no speech concerning those I have brought forward. Yet when Lord Butzer in past days brought forward Eusebius and Augustine, I let that remain for a while concerning John in the … chapter.

Ecolampadius.
