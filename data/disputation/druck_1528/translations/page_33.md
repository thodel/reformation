Since after the coming the head of the churches may be taken, as there is wisdom and emptiness.  
This, however, is precisely a matter as one who wishes to preserve it, as once a characteristic of the king was heard, that therefore he would be king; as a king should the right and the ... announce, those are also his counsel and societies: they may, however, not be called king thereby, but only the one who possesses the full power to govern.  
Thus we learn that wisdom is a characteristic of the head, but not a material one; that was ... therefore the head may be taken.  
Paul calls Christ therein the head of the churches, that he makes them alive by his Spirit and likewise ... as my brother Berchtold has said at the thirtieth ... Such power none now has, except Christ alone and all that Saint Peter or any apostle has possessed; it is nothing more than the outward preaching of the Gospel, which, however, is powerless where it does not make the one master, Christ, alive and strong in the heart.  
Therefore your Lord must bring the apostle Peter’s ... and work ... as far as it stretches, that he may therefore be called the head of the churches according to the Scripture.  
He writes a service in 1 Corinthians 4:8 and recognises, therefore, Peter a servant of the churches, makes him therefore not a head: then, according to Paul, the head of the churches is ... as we are drawn by Paul to Ephesians in the fifth chapter.
