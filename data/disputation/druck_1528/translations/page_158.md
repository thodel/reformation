The first to give belief in God’s word. Thus it would be far better if a church of Bern had certainly received the word of God from its preachers, as many have also heard, and further inquired; yet nevertheless they have been seen, and others have also been eager to hear about it. This dispute has been taken up, not that anyone may teach us (as if our four thousand were turned, as all learned and pious, then Saint Paul) in divine matters, may be learned, then openly by the preachers. Then in the other teaching there is one as the other, in the Epistle to the Corinthians, chapter V. Except alone we may openly refer to ourselves there, where it is said by Paul, the spiritual [thing] on page 28. He judges everything; he may be whoever he wishes, and may be as much or as little as he wishes. It also does not have the opinion in matters of belief, as in our command, God has openly given a thing to all the world to believe; therefore, if he is a shepherd, that is openly revealed; so it must be sealed in his heart, that all first of the Lord must believe; otherwise it will become miserable. The church of Bern must believe for itself, that the word of God is known, what others also hold; they invented in it the will of God; thus the whole world is guilty, therefore they accept; the church of Bern therefore judges that no one else has any [right], except God. Yet however our opponent does not wish to believe the word of God carried by us; then perhaps a Peter or Jacob would be a true apostle of Christ; we would gladly value our opponent’s [argument], also gladly hear such [argument] to come to fall; where are they? Then Lord Daniel says the bishop is thus [appointed]; it is not enough; he also must have Saint Peter’s [authority]; then the natural man understands divine things not to Corin, in the first Epistle, at the other chapter.
