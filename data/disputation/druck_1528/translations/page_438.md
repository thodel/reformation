The seven  

Matth. in chapter viii says to Peter that he has not found a whole people believing.  
So he spoke to him: “O you little believer, why have you forced [it]; just as a man may believe, but not so strongly that he in belief may be a whole people.”  

When Matth. in chapter xix speaks of the youth who had kept all vows, Christ then spoke to him: “Will you be perfect? Go and sell everything you have and give it to the poor.”  

Here it is a matter of belief when the youth, having died for all principal sins, would not be condemned, nor thereafter be perfectly holy; and if he were not condemned, he might be redeemed for the remainder and thereafter retain that we may be forgiven, according to Psalm lxv, as he says.  

We have passed through the water and the fire, and you have led us into the river, the same Hieremius.  

On the thirtieth day they will come into the meadow, and I will lead them again in mercy, and will lead them to the harvest, through the tumult of the water in the right way, which leads to eternal life: I do not find that one through the pain; it may be water or the river, and into eternal life will be led there by the flood.
