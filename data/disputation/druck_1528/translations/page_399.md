**Schlußred.**

so many anti‑Christian abuses are polluted / how preserved is / in the schoolmaster himself partly known / how could they not be a horror before God: So the night‑sign of the Lord / the death of Christ is highly esteemed by the believers: they have testimony of the holy Spirit / which spirit / of the diminution of Christ’s suffering in the Mass gives no testimony.

**Dannberger.**

[D]Jewyl of that night‑sign of Christ is called Cena / and thanksgiving / so it is also called a Testament of Christ. Matt. xxvj. Since it is now called a Testament / a living Testament has a faithful Executor / which the Apostles have fully carried out and fulfilled the Testament of Christ / I preserve through the holy Scripture John xix. Thus Christ on the Cross has given his Testament / and his mother Mary, behold, John, and spoken. Ecce mater tua / now was your mother / says the text at the same time / and at this hour / he has taken her into his care. There it is clearly evident / that this is the beulch of Christ. John thus shall be a guardian of Mary.

**Zwingly**

[S]It is clearly enough shown that the Testament which God has made for us / is the complete forgiveness of our sin: and as every Testament / in power and being comes / when he dies / the one who made the Testament, that thus Christ Jesus, the Son of God, has died / and the Testament testifies.
