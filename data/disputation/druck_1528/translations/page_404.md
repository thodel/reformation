The sixth to this. The last mandate of my gracious lords, which then is Christian and divine, hold such not in, therefore I am not steadfast in it. Only that one should preach what can be brought forth with divine truthful and biblical Scripture; thereby he proves his preaching, also the book of the Maccabees, indeed a biblical Scripture. For the Jew does not otherwise, but that one should call upon God who dwells in heaven. As also the prophet David does, in the hundred and twenty‑second Psalm, when he speaks. To you, Lord, I have lifted my eyes, who dwells in the heavens; also Christ teaches us to pray. Matthäus v. Our Father, who art in heaven. So now, having his part, has the learned one of his soul‑creation; I also will gladly do so for my Christian brothers and lord here.  

Herr Johannes Lottstetter
