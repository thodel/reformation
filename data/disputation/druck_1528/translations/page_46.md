Berchtoldus.  
Your name, Sir Jesus, the venerable gracious wise Lord—formerly I first reported that Lord Berchtold had said that the servitude is half or a servant, as they take on service modestly and responsibly. A king also does this, but he is not yet a king. It is neither necessary nor impossible that he has brought it about, if indeed one is, or is to be made a superior, or a subordinate, a very noble one against a certain … A duke in a kingdom may be appointed a subordinate. He is also a superior, a very noble king; thus he is a subordinate: a very noble one toward his subordinates, so he is a superior and may be appointed a head; that is one.  

As for the other point, concerning me, it is not possible for me, I do not believe that the few possible orderly things in this matter can be arranged now; the foundations are in the matters that follow, so that I cannot well retain them. Yet, if Lord Berchtold opens them again to me, I will answer thereafter.
