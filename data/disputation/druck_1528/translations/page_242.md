**Die vierte**  
In the past, from what He gives, as in the present, and from what He will give, as in the future, as it has happened after the night‑sign. Because now these words of John in chapter V and the words of the night‑sign are so closely compared with one another, we may therefore find no gloss according to the content of the mandate, only the true word of God. Yet if we should need a gloss here, many would be taken, namely the glosses of Origen, Cyprian, Hilary, Jerome, Ambrose, Cyril, Chrysostom, Theophylact, who all unanimously hold that the words of John in chapter V concerning the sacrament of the Eucharist serve together with the whole Christian Church. This is my opinion about the words of John in chapter V.  

**Ecolampadius.**  
[Über] Schülmeyster: By the words of John concerning the bread that gives us life, bringing forth our whole need contrary to that, as greatly and powerfully shown by the words of John.  
One has not spoken about how the name of the bread may be taken in many ways; we would also like to show besides that the breads which have been made manifold, of which also in the chapter are written.  
In sum, the Lord wishes to be asked of us alone, as the life‑giving bread that strengthens us, so that we may recognize how He gave His life for us in death.  
From the sacramental bread, still to be preserved with godly Scripture. You would like that we are not gathered because of the teachers.
