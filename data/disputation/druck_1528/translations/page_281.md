Lord Benedict lets altogether with lofty such  
words / according to the words of the Lord at night‑times / and yet he has not yet explained such things / that they may serve him: therefore we must forgive him the ... as futile words. But according to the saying in the first Epistle to the Corinthians at v. ca. and v. the seventh ca. Exodus I give such an answer / this does not ... / then it does not refer to the sacramental bread.  

Thus Paul here wishes to instruct the people / which in the belief altogether Christ was the Easter lamb offered on the cross for us / must be eaten: and not only at the time of receiving the sacrament. Such a people he wants that they be altogether pure / that from them the open sins be removed / which are compared with the sour / or the consecrated bread.  

Just as the old ... lovingly ate the Easter lamb with unblemished bread / so he wants / that we in our belief / in Christ / have fellowship with those who are an unblemished bread.  

But Lord Benedict says / how the figure there is depicted / so equally ... it pleases me well:  

Thus if he wishes to compare ceremony with ceremony / so have the old ... the Easter lamb lovingly eaten with the thanksgiving of his redemption.  

Thus also we hold our ceremonies and our service / in the breaking of the sacramental bread / with thanksgiving of our redemption. They are sure, however, that through the ... the redemption ... is in Christ himself fulfilled on the cross and through our belief.
