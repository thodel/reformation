Adeyster Ulrich zwingy.  

When there was a great distinction of faith, more or less given, we recognize well: but this argument or counter‑argument was directed solely at the greatness of faith and not at the power of the watery word; for faith is able, not the watery word. Thus we are right, and they are refuted by that saying, that the watery word should have such power, as they show by wondrous works, the works of Peter, which have not been wrought through me, the believers. I say that the speech confirms our former opinion; then the miracles still stood in my faith and in the watery word; therefore my opinion is again light; then the strength of the miracles is not in our belief, but in the power of God alone; therefore here it does not happen, but after necessity it is to be torn apart; and now those who speak of Christ from strong faith do not speak against it.  

Pfarrer von Sant Gallen.  

We know well that the word of man, as one man, does not harm: but as we act against the blemish of the word of God, it does not act only as a natural thing, but as a supernatural one; it opens itself in the word; so Peter answered the Lord; the governor, working through the whole night, we have caught nothing; any word, however, I will spread the net: as they should have it, they have closed it; a great multitude of fish. Thus it should be drawn forward; the saying in the first Epistle of Peter, chapter one, and to the Romans, at the ninth Epistle.
