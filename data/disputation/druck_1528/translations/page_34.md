Die erst  

The lord Martin Buger had a long discourse and a written work made to give an example of a king who possesses great power in his kingdom; he also possessed the knowledge of the law, statutes, and orders, as he then took that same knowledge or recognized the statutes of law. He also had fine doctors or councilors, but this does not make the king be taken. Now it is also here that one may believe that Saint Peter may preach or teach, that Saint Peter has taught, but that therefore a chief may not be taken, for lack of the power to rule. If I write thereby that Saint Peter received the authority from Christ here on earth over the Christian Church, and that this is not for himself alone, but for eternity for himself and his descendants. If I do that, then afterwards I will see about a little—
