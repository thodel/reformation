Johannes Büchstab  

Abel has offered from the firstborn of his … .  
Berchtoldus Haller.  

The schoolmaster has answered: Abel has offered. / It seems to me / Abel is better than his … . / Now he is inclined toward death, / that he is better than the death‑giver, / but this is not invented from Scripture, / because death is an essential element. / Accordingly Mary has not offered Christ, / but, as was said in the temple, / they have indeed offered a few things, / but not Christ, / as is spoken of here.  

Johannes Büchstab  

Already Abel is better than that offering; / the same therefore need not be everywhere.  
Say further about the beginning that Lord Berch… / introduced, that the Mass is a horror before God, / because of the addition of ceremonies, / cruets, / vestments, / persons. / I am not yet sufficient with Scripture in my instruction.  
The costly Mass‑… the half / we have a uniform prelude in the … as Exodus 28 and 35, 39. / And as Mary Magdalene the believers in Christ, / with … visible works, has constituted. / Luke at 5 …  

Thus also a contemporary Christian may, according to his will, serve God with useful things, / gifts and ceremonies, / because in them … great misuse.
