**Conclusion Speech CLXIX**  
***Aechster Vlrich forcibly.***  

[A]ll our doctrine and teaching is also only of the same / therefore we let all Christians recognize.  

***Herr Matthias, Pastor of Söngen, of the Anzug***  
[H]des Comenthůrs zu Kussnacht, as Collator and Feudal lords of the named parish of Söngen, concerning the account of his teaching, has the same Lord Matthias openly declared that he has preached the Gospel and has abolished all ceremonies as far as possible. But concerning the Sacrament, he has from the Evangelists Mattheo, Marco, Luca, Johanne and Paulo not understood the letters otherwise, and thus preached as the pastor of Sant Gallen: And as he himself reports, also through Meyster Vlrich and others reports: he needs the explanation well. And as he earlier from the first hand was present, also now by this vest and handcraft his, so much God’s help is concealed. ꝛc.  

***Conradus Som of Rotenacker***  
***Preacher of Vlm.***  

[F]Romen Christian, dear gentlemen and brothers, I am not of the opinion here that I would refute the conclusion speech, rather I hold it to be Christian and in God’s Scripture so well founded that neither the devil nor any man can oppose it. Therefore I have preached the same to a Christian congregation at Vlm, and especially the fourth conclusion speech, which contains the sign of the Lord, namely that the body and the blood of Christ in bread and wine will not be essentially received.
