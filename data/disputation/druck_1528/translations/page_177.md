**Martinus Butzer.**  

I refer myself to the Acts of this appointed censor at 65, the sayings themselves. Acts in the fifteenth chapter. Folio.  

**Theobaldus Hüter.**  

It is well done for me and I give this to a mild later, whether the statutes of the churches have not been above the spirit. Together I wish to conclude briefly and humbly on behalf of my lords the presidents. But yesterday, at length, I presented an example of the unmeasured young woman Maria, as if one had unjustly added to her. After that, and I understand, I therefore say that I, together with my ... (spiritual) ... recognize Mary, the Mother of God, that she is a young woman before birth, after birth, and in birth pure and undefiled.  

**Zwingli.**  

Dear Sir Pastor, so that the matter may be shortened, understand us thus. The other closing speech has two points, explained thereafter the former and rests only on the first. Whoever ... thus ... there is no objection to any church being allowed in pressing matters, according to circumstance, to agree on praying, fasting, giving alms, and other divine works. Example: Where hunger, death, war (upon which God may keep us) arise, the church may at Bâlp and Bollingen, from each, come together, ... (symbol).
