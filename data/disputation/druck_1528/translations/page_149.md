**Zwingli.**  

[D]er God Lord still does not recognize / that indeed three persons are in the divinity / the three persons / are not the same one single God / and that so is spoken by the Father / or by the Son / or by the Holy Spirit / not the same under one person / which is understood as one God / there is no need to speak longer about it / for it would be altogether too late / that in the laudable disputation / such suspicious arguments should be allowed / those who understand the unity of the one God / count or make much of it / therefore the three persons are / etc.  
   Indeed such an argument is untenable / all because he does not show us / that one was truly God and man / so remains forever and ever the one Christ / the one head of the Church / even if we alike this word / had never been judged from the Scripture here / which, however, completely occurred in the Acts on the 17th leaf.
