The first peoples, united in the year, have received, and so soon among us the divine Word, through the pious call, has been broken in high places; the old friend has brought his old pieces against them and has caused many sects and division, namely that the freedom of the Word has been lost. In other places he, with worldly force, has twisted the Word, under open and white (i.e., pure) pretenses; he yet, according to the course of the Gospel, by us, where he the worldly powers offer the preaching office, may not bring it; he moves this year a confused people, the unbridled separation, that God therefore will have, that they may learn not from any man but from the assurance of the holy Spirit inward in the heart. Every one must be taught by God and in his heart thus assured, that he—not only a council, not only the whole world, but also the angels from heaven with signs and wonders—may give another (i.e., another teaching), that he with Paul may curse. Thus I have, beloved pious Christians, answered the admonitions of the provincial, driven away the whole spirit; that is, every Christian should have all things set right. As for me, that I shall no longer be responsible, he brings Scripture; so he finds answer; he brings his own speech; so he finds silence; but nevertheless, over love, after the battle of human speech, his heart may be illuminated by divine truth.
