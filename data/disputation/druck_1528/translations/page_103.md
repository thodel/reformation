**Meiſter Vlrich Zwingli/**  
Predicant zu Zürich.  

So my lord of Zürich, the so‑old noble city, which has held itself so piously, wisely and faithfully, may it be in peace or in times of war especially, they are drawn together; they are led astray by me, although such would be justly avoided; yet it is not a place so praiseworthy, and the foremost of the Confederation remains unanswered.  On that I say that the named my gracious lords, through me not sufficiently led astray, also do not trust me, but according to what they are my … the not mine, but God’s … .  Just as the … in Berœa.
