**Schlußred XXVII**  
Worthy of the Spirit of God is that no artificial or earthly means can be set up or imposed, and therefore the very same time of the Spirit of God cannot turn against itself. Which also the fruits of that spirit show, whereby the receding wolves are recognized; so then the community is concluded in all power, especially the power of Christ. Yet this matter is not to be concealed; we ask Doctor Conrad Treiger to gather for himself the various moving points and, on that occasion, after a decisive decision as soon as possible, to address the same, so that we may meet each other in the same shape.  

**Doctor Conrad Treiger**  
Provincial.  

From the dear beloved Christians, from my inner sacred heart, I have undertaken, against both first closing speeches of the opposition, an answer to Doctor Fabricius Capito and to the congregation, how a two‑fold Christian church is, namely a spiritual one, which is in the spirit, which is the body of Christ the Lord, and also alone governed by the Lord; such churches no one may judge or overturn; then the Lord. As Paul shows, when he says, “The righteous shall be judged by no one; but He judges all things; my Lord Doctor has therefore concluded that the righteous, if he may judge all things, also the Scripture should be judged; so far it has been spoken by them, namely that Scripture is your judge; Paul, however, wishes to say here, “The righteous will not be judged, for there is no …”
