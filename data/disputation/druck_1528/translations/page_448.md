The seven and you would not lie about the debt / What else follows from this / nor that the judge should cast you into the [gfencknuß] / until you have paid the debt / until you have done so promptly. This is now the example / that Christ teaches us: So when his brother insults him / he incurs guilt against him. Therefore he should be another / that he unite himself with him / so that the insult will not be brought before the supreme judge / God / in the self‑same one speaks: “You slavish servant, I have forgiven you all your debt / because you begged me: should you not also have mercy on your fellow servant / as I have had mercy on you.” And therefore the judge will cast you into the eternal church. Thus, with the likeness of the right / that the debtor must provide eternal [gfencknuß] / He commands us, the Lord, / that we agree with our neighbor / so that we do not fall into eternal wrath and punishment. And here … nothing is said by the judge / except much more thereafter.
