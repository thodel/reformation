[A] As Herr Marti Butzer has confessed / the Sunday and twelve days / have been set aside / from eight churches / asks me / when all that / so the church holds / and with ... against the word of God it is not to be overlooked / then St. Paul to the Philippians at iiij (4) also writes. / So he thinks something else / that also will be revealed by God / I understand thus / that the Christian church gives God the Almighty something to honor and to praise and ordains / that such, in the power of this word, Christianly may exist / also because he in iiij (4) cap. of the same epistle also writes. / Dear brothers, what is true / what is shameful / what is just / what is holy or pure / what is beautiful / what is a good lamp / is something of a virtue, etc. / In the power of these two speeches of Paul / all good Christian orders consisted as a decree of the fast‑days / feast‑days / song / also the glorious cross‑procession / in which serious deeds / behaviors / and thanksgivings for all people occur. / (cf. Timothy i) at ij (1).  

Martinus Butzer.
