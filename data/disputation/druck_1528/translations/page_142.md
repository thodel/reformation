LXI Die erste  

The blood‑shedding and dying, and thus Christ was not before the Church, when Christ spoke to John in the nineteenth chapter on the Cross. All is accomplished, and his spirit gave up, and out of his sides water and blood flowed; this is for the old sinful water a pure new measure, a spiritual water, that is the living Christian Church formed, to the Epistles at the fifth chapter. Through Christ our Lord, by his blood‑shedding and dying, as it is shown to John in the twelfth chapter.  

Thus Christ says: Let it then be seen that the grain of the wheat, if it falls into the earthly death, will bring no fruit. The grain is Christ, and when the grain dies, it will bring much fruit; this is the life of all Christians.  

Item: in the Acts of the Apostles, chapter twenty, he overcame the Church with his blood.  

Item: to the Colossians, chapter nine. God, who raised Jesus from the dead, also made him life‑giving.  

Item: to the Ephesians, chapter ten. We have our redemption in Christ through his blood.  

Item: in the first Epistle of Peter, chapter ten. Christ has redeemed us in his blood; with this I think I have shown enough that this is our living Christian Church, not only according to divinity but also according to humanity, and through his bitter suffering, blood‑shedding and dying, may it be thus martyred and justified; then the Church, so before the future Christ, is not such a living form.  

Thus all members of the same Church were still in the wrath of God and not yet granted salvation, but those there perished, all in the prisons of the pre‑hell.  

Berchtoldus
