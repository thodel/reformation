**Closing Speech**  
**Explanation of D. Johannis Ecolam‑**  
**padij of the place Danieli on the 4th.**  

So, if one thoroughly seeks the Scripture, it has the meaning: it is spoken in the utterance of the watchmen and was asked for in the conversation of the saints. It is evident, yet it is not a report of the prayer that has been cut off; and this argument serves no purpose here in the foundation: then the prophet will say alone that the matter has already been spoken concerning King Nebuchadnezzar.  

**Büchstab**  

Since Christ is our sole mediator and teaches us to pray for one another, it is proper that the dead are not cut off, for there is no cheerful Scripture concerning this. The saying of Daniel shows us that he descended from heaven: he cannot well serve a living one. Saint Peter also spoke. i.e. Peter. I will, however, diligently anchor that I may have you eternally after my death, that you may keep the memory of all these things. Here Saint Peter has engaged, with one, something to deal with after his death.  

**Berchtoldus**  

I ask whether you acknowledge that Christ is a sufficient mediator.  

**Büchstab**  

Yes, I acknowledge it.  

**Berchtoldus**  

It is sufficient to show that Christ is our sole mediator; therefore we need no other mediator.
