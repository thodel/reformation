Schlußred.  
The pastor personally resisted / in all articles of the concluding speeches / the will of our great Lord / and in his mind opposed the counter‑argument with a clear word of God / rejecting or overturning it / however our dear Lord’s brothers, the preachers of this praised place Bern, together with other learned men / have so far been more sufficient and skillful in their conduct / that we could have given them the honor gladly / and would gladly remain by the same.  

With such an attachment / that we here hopefully have addressed and protested / that we may hold the ten concluding speeches for the Christian faith / founded in Scripture / and keep them in belief, preaching / also with the help of God, further with flow and earnestness to be done. In which acquaintance, in equal measure, stands Herr Mathias Kessler, pastor of Geyß in Apenzell, our brother / who, for his own reasons, has withdrawn his church here against the state / and would be ready to give an account of his faith and preaching / so that anyone may be thus instructed.  

We also would like to suffer generally / the pastor let his writing be badly composed / therefore he stopped / and gave himself over to the holding word of God. In which we, in a fitting manner, should agree / as also the one blessed and beloved of our Lord, the Obern of Apenzell, is / and the one cause why we are sent here / the Lord gives grace / his holy, eternal, and uncontradictable word / our only holy and comfort / faithfully to encourage the faithful / that the name of God may be broken not now nor forever. Amen.
