**Pfarrer von Sant gallen.**  
Vynzug and responsibility of our fellow brothers /  
is human knowledge / that (if God wills) we are of Christ /  
and thus concerning his word we speak. Were we heathen or unbelieving,  
we would speak of Machameto or Joue. Then I, the confessed Christians,  
who with the heart believe in righteousness, and such with the mouth  
confess, … and also briefly concerning the Acts.  

**Zwingli.**  
From this answer we say thus / that certainly we know / no one would  
wish to know whether he is the light of Christ’s grace or not; then we  
simply could not know whether such a word speaks / whether one has  
believed from the heart of Christ or not. Therefore, my dear brothers, I  
ask: Do you have a right, unforced belief in the living Son of God,  
Christ Jesus?  

**Pfarrer von Sant gallen.**  
If God wills, yes.
