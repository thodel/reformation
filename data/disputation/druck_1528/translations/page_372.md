And to add, I have just now said that it must not be mixed with an open script; let it remain.  
Further answers from Lord Berchtold’s entourage: The words of Christ I have spoken: “Thus that in my memory …” I find something reported in the Hebrew language, that to do, also to offer, is called.  
But if I set such things learned from the Hebrew to [the] context, then the very language is unknown to me.  
Butzer.+  

Thus the Lord says: “Thus to me this is remembered as useless; just hold that the deed is not called offering, as also explained by Paul in Corinth sufficiently, for he says: So often you eat from the bread … and … as the Scripture … something for offering is taken … so rather than that which one offers as a sacrifice or the like. Here, however, hold that the deed is called eating.” The addition has already been announced, that it is mixed with the holy Scripture, says Lord Berchtold in his declaration concerning this concluding speech.  
Letter  

DJewyl jr has left: that “Facere”, also something, is called “offering”, is also to be added here.  
Butzer  

What is this as a consequence? “Facere” is called something at a place because of a promised wording of offering; therefore it must also be called offering here, since clearly the opposite is printed.
