D. Ecolampadius  

It has been brought forward / from the ix chapter of the prophet Zechariah / a saying with such words: “And also you / in the blood of your testimony / have released your prisoners / from the cisterns / in which there is no water.” This saying shows us how / through Christ / we are freed from a heavy bondage of sin / which bondage is compared / to a cistern / or a stagnant pool / where your water and comfort are present / and it does not actually pertain to the absent punishment / after death: because we also / through the death of Christ / are freed from the eternal bright enlightenment; then the merciful God / having taken such oppressive labor from us / and we, Christ, having been planted through faith / are, by the worthiness of the service / of the blood‑sacrifice of Jesus Christ / after death / the believers / still have neither share nor punishment remaining.  

It has also been brought forward / another saying / Job x. / which is by no means contrary to us / but rather much more for us. So Job begs: “Leave me / that I may no longer endure my sorrow / that I may go away / and not return to the dark earth.”
