**Schlußrede.**  
The others speak all together, that they are not preserving / that another deserves or such likeness enough / then Christ / so they need your answer: they are sufficiently explained in the proof of this article / as Berchtold has given before. But that we ourselves should not choose / otherwise patiently receive what / God sends to us / also preserve sufficiently the saying / drawn from himself / Matthäus chapter x vj. capitel. That we ourselves should deny. Now we can no longer act according to our own will. The selected castrations are from Paul rejected / to be collated in the other chapter.  

**Buchstab**  
[D] The Scripture, introduced by me up to now, / by the same I let it now remain / deny not Christ / a complete sufficiency for all the world’s sin / but with the judgment / that we ourselves of the same sufficiency of Christ / also make partakers / as I have partly shown.  

**Butzer.**  
[G] God be praised / that the schoolmaster / Christ Jesus acknowledges our complete sufficiency: then / it is a complete sufficiency / so we will not be able further to act with our works. But that he adds the judgment / we must make ourselves partakers of his sufficiency / saying we / that so‑thing in God / and not our hand instead. Believing we in our Lord / so he is our whole …
